About Us

Why families trust Mavis Cares & Nurses

Our Story - Named After My Mother, Built with Heart

Mavis Cares & Nurses was born from a daughter's love. Named after my mother, Mavis - a woman who taught me that caring is not a job, it's a calling. We started with one heart: to give every family in Gauteng the kind of care we would want for our own family members. Reliable, affordable, & dignified home care where your loved one is treated as the family they are, not a patient. Based in Lenasia South, we now serve Johannesburg, Pretoria & surrounding areas - with police-cleared, trained caregivers available 24/7.
