About Mavis Cares & Nurses

Compassionate care you can trust in Pretoria

Who We Are


Mavis Cares & Nurses is a trusted home-based care agency providing professional caregivers, nurses, and support staff for families across Gauteng.


Founded with love and a passion for caring, we understand that leaving your loved one in someone else's hands is a big decision. That's why we only send trained, vetted, and compassionate carers.


Our Mission


To provide affordable, reliable, and dignified care that allows our elderly, sick, and vulnerable to live comfortably at home.


Why Families Choose Us


  • ✔ Police cleared and referenced carers
  • ✔ Trained in first aid and elderly care
  • ✔ Available 24/7 - day and night shifts
  • ✔ Short-term and long-term care
